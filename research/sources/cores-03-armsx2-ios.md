## Before you start

- iPhone or iPad on iOS 17 or later. Without JIT most games are too slow to play.
- You provide your own PS2 BIOS and game files. Neither is included.
- Performance varies by device and by game. Some run perfectly, others crawl or will not boot. Check the [compatibility list](https://armsx2.net/compatibility) first.

## Two channels

### ARMSX2 iOS

The tested release. Start here.

![](https://ios.armsx2.net/assets/icon-nightly.png?v=1ce15bef)

### ARMSX2 Nightly

Fresh code, most days. New features arrive here first, and so do the bugs.

They install side by side, with separate save states, memory cards and settings.
Trying a nightly leaves your stable install untouched.
